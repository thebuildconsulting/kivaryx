# Kivaryx Series 01 — locked and working decisions

**Locked by creator direction, 16 Nov 2026**

- THE BUILD is a **consulting-led, deeply educational engineering series** that aims to demonstrate how our team assesses, explains, decides and implements infrastructure changes.
- First fictional company is fintech with both transaction and AI/ML use cases. Provisional brand **Kivaryx**; do not assert exclusive rights or availability.
- Shared, persistent story and working product across playlist; no disposable unrelated scenarios.
- Every episode closes with a useful, client-ready consulting report: executive summary + evidence + options + business impact + recommendation + outcomes. Final episode synthesizes the series.
- Technology education is integrated into each justified change; audience should understand concepts, architecture, alternatives and tradeoffs, not simply watch commands.
- Introduce AWS services (including Bedrock, SageMaker AI, EKS Auto Mode, CloudWatch/X-Ray) and evolving open source (Cilium, Gateway API, OpenTelemetry) selectively in response to established needs; compare with making no change or configuring the existing system.
- Series length and video runtime flexible; no hard 20-minute ceiling for this playlist. Original distinct 24-video Kubernetes Networking syllabus remains unchanged.
- All lab assets, transaction records, finances and incidents synthetic; no real bank/card/customer data or employer infrastructure. No AWS spending or external model APIs without separate approval.

**Working design to validate later:** 12 chapters, $18k illustrative monthly cloud spend, 12 fictional engineers, AWS managed node groups, fraud scoring on CPUs and a read-only operator AI proof of concept. Product prototype uses a local monolith now and is to be expanded by Codex.
