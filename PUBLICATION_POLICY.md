# Public release and confidentiality policy

- **Kivaryx is fictional**. Mark repository, videos, website pages and reports clearly as synthetic engineering demonstrations. Do not market as an anonymised real client.
- The company story is a simulation. Do not imply THE BUILD audited or remediated a real fintech.
- Real client work belongs to approved **Use Cases** on thebuildconsulting.com and requires publication rights and accurate attribution.
- Do not commit database files, access keys, `.env`, private credentials, Terraform state, cloud IDs from real accounts, raw video recordings, logs with tokens, or real payment data.
- All intentionally vulnerable scenarios must be opt-in, locally isolated and reversible. Never publish exploitable exposed infrastructure.
- Public repository visibility is owner-approved as of 16 November 2026. Review source, commit history, GitHub Actions logs and artifacts before any separately authorized upload/release to this public repository. Visibility approval does not authorize pushes, tags, publication of evidence/media, or exposure of the running demo.
- Confirm licences for source, docs, diagrams and media, including third-party materials; choose public licence before publishing.
- Do not make fake savings, real-world performance, certifications or compliance claims. Measured lab outcomes require evidence, sample counts and setup info; dollar figures are illustrative until linked to real approved cost data.
- Public site must link to *verified published Git tag/release*, not an untested moving branch.
- No push to remote, no visibility changes, no GitHub/org settings or live deployments without explicit approval at that stage.
